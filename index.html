Overview

Bridging the Digital Divide
in Agricultural Markets

Bangladesh's agricultural sector — valued at USD 47.54 billion — is critically hampered by information asymmetry. Over 85% of farmers depend on intermediaries who capture up to 40% of retail prices, leaving farmers earning 20–30% below market value.

Post-harvest losses range from 23.6% to 43.5%, often a direct consequence of poor market access and a lack of timely information. Existing digital solutions consistently fail rural farmers with low digital literacy.

Agri-VoiceLink addresses this through a multimodal conversational interface accepting spoken Bangla and images of handwritten market boards — making real-time price discovery and supply-chain intelligence accessible to every farmer, regardless of literacy level.

Information Asymmetry

Syndicate intermediaries consistently suppress farmgate prices and inflate retail prices. Agri-VoiceLink's GNN engine detects and exposes these price manipulation networks in real time.

Digital Inclusion by Design

Voice input in native Bangla dialects and OCR for handwritten text ensure that non-literate and low-literacy farmers are not left behind by the digital transition.

Transparent Digital Marketplace

Beyond price data, Agri-VoiceLink creates a competing transparent network — a structural intervention that directly undermines syndicate market power.

Specific Objectives

Three Pillars of the Project

Each objective is structured with measurable performance indicators and sequential milestones.

SO — 01

Technical Development & Model Validation

Build and validate the hybrid LLM-GNN architecture integrating specialized Bangla ASR and OCR modules capable of analyzing and disseminating agricultural market dynamics at scale.

Target: >95% ASR & OCR accuracy
SO — 02

Farmer Empowerment & Access

Conduct a structured pilot program onboarding 5,000 farmers across two distinct agricultural districts, ensuring an intuitive, frictionless experience for non-digital-native users.

Target: 5,000 farmers onboarded
SO — 03

Market Impact & Economic Metrics

Quantitatively demonstrate platform impact through measurable increases in gross farmer income and verifiable reduction in post-harvest losses by project completion.

Target: >10% income increase
Technical Architecture

A Hybrid AI Engine for
Agricultural Intelligence

The platform's innovation lies in combining the conversational fluency of LLMs with the network-analysis power of GNNs — designed specifically for the complexity of agricultural supply chains.

🧠

Large Language Model (LLM)

A fine-tuned open-source LLM serves as the conversational front-end, processing natural-language queries in spoken or written Bangla and translating them into structured data requests for the analytical engine.

Conversational Interface
🕸️

Graph Neural Network (GNN)

The analytical core models the entire supply chain as a complex graph — farmers, markets, commodities, buyers as nodes; transactions and goods flow as edges. Detects syndicate sub-graphs and price manipulation patterns invisible to traditional methods.

Market Intelligence
🎙️

Bangla ASR Pipeline

Built on a fine-tuned Whisper-based model trained on a custom corpus of agricultural vocabulary and local dialects, achieving >95% speech-to-text accuracy across a diverse farmer demographic.

Voice Input · Bangla
📷

Bangla OCR Pipeline

A modified ResNet-34 architecture handles handwritten Bangla market board images through binarization, character segmentation, and classification — achieving >97% accuracy on handwritten characters and numerics.

Image Input · Handwritten
Project Roadmap

Two-Year, Eight-Quarter Plan

Year one focuses on research and development; year two on pilot deployment and commercialization.

Year 1 · Q1–Q2 · Jan–Jun 2026

Foundation & Data Acquisition

Literature review of Bangla OCR/ASR and GNNs. Field interviews with farmers. Collection of historical market price data and annotated voice/handwritten datasets for model fine-tuning.

Technical Specification Doc Annotated ASR/OCR Dataset
Year 1 · Q3–Q4 · Jul–Dec 2026

Hybrid Model & Prototype Development

Core LLM-GNN architecture development. Training and fine-tuning of Bangla ASR and OCR modules. Integration and internal testing of functional alpha prototype.

Alpha Prototype Model Technical Documentation
Year 2 · Q5–Q6 · Jan–Jun 2027

Pilot Deployment & Optimization

Onboard and train 5,000 farmers in two districts. Deploy beta platform. Collect user feedback, transactional data, and analyze market syndicate patterns in real-world conditions.

Pilot Launch Report Refined Beta Platform
Year 2 · Q7–Q8 · Jul–Dec 2027

Impact Assessment & Commercialization

Final quantitative and qualitative analysis of pilot data. Develop scalable commercialization strategy. Establish formal partnerships for post-grant sustainability and nationwide scale.

Final Impact Report Business & Sustainability Plan
